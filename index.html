The Journey

OUR STORY

Samuel Graphix was born from a simple but powerful conviction: that every brand deserves world-class design — regardless of where it's built.

Founded by Ikenna Samuel, a digital designer with roots in both creative arts and clinical science, the studio bridges two worlds that rarely meet — technical precision and visual storytelling.

Currently based in Nigeria and serving clients across health, media, and technology sectors, Samuel Graphix brings a rare blend of strategic thinking and artistic courage to every brief.

2018
The Beginning. Ikenna picks up his first design tool — and never puts it down.
2020
Going deeper. Formal studies in Clinical Lab Technology sharpen a unique analytical approach to creative work.
2022
Studio founded. Samuel Graphix is established with a clear vision: design that means something.
2024
Industry recognition. Joins Willis Work Engineering Services as Digital Designer, delivering high-impact content at scale.
2026
Building forward. Expanding into health, media, and tech sectors — one iconic project at a time.
